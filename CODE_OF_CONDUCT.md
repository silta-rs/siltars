# Code of Conduct

Silta contributors are expected to keep project spaces professional, respectful,
and focused on the work.

## Expected Behavior

- Be direct without being hostile.
- Critique ideas and code, not people.
- Assume good intent while still asking for technical clarity.
- Keep discussions relevant to the project.
- Respect maintainer decisions after tradeoffs have been considered.

## Unacceptable Behavior

- Harassment, threats, or personal attacks.
- Discriminatory language or behavior.
- Sustained off-topic disruption.
- Publishing private information without permission.

## Reporting

Report conduct issues privately to the maintainers through the security contact
or a project maintainer address once one is published.

Maintainers may remove comments, close discussions, restrict participation, or
take other action needed to keep the project healthy.
